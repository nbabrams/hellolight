the team

Experts in their craft, world changers in their own right.

Each team member shares a desire to empower nonprofits, and brings their own special something to make these projects come to life.

  • Nick Abrams

    Creative Director

    Nick has spent over 18 years bringing creative projects to life for clients like San Diego Rescue Mission, The Cause, North Coast Calvary Chapel, Christian Surfers, Unite San Diego, North America Sport Movement, Mesa Farm, Degree 33 Surfboards, Sonos, Kashi, PayPal, Toyota, and Patagonia.

  • Greg Helton

    Greg is the founder and lead strategist at Larimore Production Company — a storyteller who uses film to build brands, promote businesses, and empower people. He also founded the Voicegivers podcast, dedicated to helping nonprofits reach their goals through education.

    larimoreproductioncompany.com ↗

  • Melana Preston

    Copywriter / Content Strategist

    Melana has sixteen years of experience providing consulting and project-based creative services for nonprofits, social enterprises, and small businesses, locally and globally.

    Editor

    James is an award-winning commercial filmmaker with 20 years in the industry. He has played a fundamental role in the growth of several studios, holding positions as creative director, production manager, and post-production supervisor.

    Developer
