what we do

One nonprofit each year. A full creative partnership, given freely.

We look for organizations that are Kingdom minded, with a clear, focused, and compelling mission — 501(c)(3) or fiscally sponsored — and an underdog: small, scrappy, eager, and underfunded.

why

The problem

Some nonprofits struggle to tell their story in a clear and compelling way.

Our answer

As creatives who want to make our world better, we use our gifts to amplify theirs. One nonprofit a year, at no cost to them.

The hope

That the work increases their funding, so they can keep making the world better for all of us.
