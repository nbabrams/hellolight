faq

Frequently asked questions.

I’m a nonprofit — how can I work with Hello Light?

Each year we interview and choose one nonprofit partner. If you’re interested, get in touch and we’ll get back to you with next steps.

How can I contribute?

The most direct way is to support the work. Another is to tell someone about it — a nonprofit, a creative, or someone who would want to back this kind of thing. You can also follow along on Instagram and LinkedIn.

I want to join the team — how does that work?

Our hope is to build a community of top-level, like-minded creatives who want to make the world a better place. If that’s you, say hello.

Are my donations tax deductible?

Yes. We partner with The Cause, a 501(c)(3), so every gift is tax deductible.

Can I copy what you’re doing in my own community?

That would be amazing. Contact us and we’ll find ways to help you make it happen.

What is the heart behind Hello Light?

We’re all made to make something. For those of us who know Jesus, that means using what we make to advance His kingdom. Hello Light connects top-level creatives with vetted nonprofits so those creatives can volunteer their craft, and the nonprofits can stay focused entirely on their mission. The creative craft may not be the light the world needs — but we can use it to fuel the ones on the front lines, so their light can burn even brighter. For the creatives, it’s a chance to be part of something meaningful, real, impactful, and eternal.
